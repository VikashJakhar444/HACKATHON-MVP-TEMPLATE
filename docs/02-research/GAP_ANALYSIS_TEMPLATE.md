## Language & Precision Guidelines
> **Important**: Do not claim an issue is an "unsolved problem" or "impossible today" unless conclusive evidence exists. Use precise, defensible descriptors:
> - *Underserved* (target group overlooked by major players)
> - *Fragmented* (requires too many disjointed tools)
> - *Inefficient / Slow* (manual steps cause latency)
> - *Costly / Inaccessible* (existing enterprise tools prohibit smaller teams)
> - *Difficult to use / High cognitive load* (poor UX creates frequent human error)
> - *Contextually insufficient* (fails under specific constraints like offline, mobile, or real-time)
