# Research Activity Log

## Purpose
This document is the permanent, append-only ledger for all targeted external research conducted during the hackathon. Every research inquiry must tie directly to a specific question, verified source, and architectural/product decision.

## Operating Rules
- **Append-Only**: Never delete, rewrite, or compress past research entries.
- **Traceability**: Link every inquiry to an affected decision (ADR) or requirement.
- **Time-Boxed**: Log entries as research progresses to maintain awareness of the 24-hour limit.

---

## Log Entry Schema
```text
[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS LOCAL TIME]

RESEARCH ID: RES-XXX
QUESTION: <precise question investigated>
REASON: <why this matters to the MVP>
SOURCE: <URL, documentation page, or primary reference>
KEY FINDING: <concise summary of findings>
CONFIDENCE: <HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW>
DECISION AFFECTED: <ADR ID, feature spec, or architecture component>
FOLLOW-UP: <None | specific next inquiry>
STATUS: <COMPLETED | IN-PROGRESS | BLOCKED | SUPERSEDED>
```
