# Pitch Deck Content Specification Template

> **Instructions**: Use this template to draft concise, high-impact slide content for the final hackathon presentation. Avoid text-heavy slides; focus on clear visual suggestions, bold takeaways, and empirical proof.

---

## Pitch Deck Principles

- **One Coherent Narrative**: Problem $\rightarrow$ Gap $\rightarrow$ Solution $\rightarrow$ Working Demo $\rightarrow$ Value.
- **Match Reality**: Every feature claimed on slides must be fully operational in the working demo.
- **Separate Future Scope**: Clearly label future concepts to preserve integrity.
- **Scannable Content**: Use punchy bullet points and strong visual suggestions rather than dense paragraphs.

---

## 13-Slide Default Deck Structure

---

### SLIDE 1: Title & Value Proposition
- **Slide Objective**: Introduce the product and grab audience attention immediately.
- **Key Message**: *[Product Name]* — *[One-Line Value Proposition]*
- **Supporting Content**: Team Name, Hackathon Track / Category.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Clean product logo, bold high-contrast title typography, modern gradient background.

---

### SLIDE 2: The Core Problem
- **Slide Objective**: Articulate the acute real-world friction.
- **Key Message**: *State the single most painful problem in 1–2 punchy sentences.*
- **Supporting Content**: 2–3 specific data points or real-world friction examples.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Problem illustration or compelling stat callout.
- **Evidence / Source**: Grounded in `docs/01-problem/PROBLEM_ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md`.

---

### SLIDE 3: Target Persona & User Pain
- **Slide Objective**: Establish who experiences the problem daily.
- **Key Message**: Who is hurting the most and what workflow bottleneck they face.
- **Supporting Content**: Primary user profile, current manual steps, time/money lost.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Persona card or "Day in the Life" before-state diagram.

---

### SLIDE 4: The Existing Gap
- **Slide Objective**: Explain why existing tools and competitors are insufficient.
- **Key Message**: Existing tools solve X, but leave Y broken for our users.
- **Supporting Content**: Breakdown of competitor limitations and the specific unaddressed gap.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Competitor comparison matrix highlighting the clear gap.
- **Evidence / Source**: Grounded in `docs/02-research/GAP_ANALYSIS_TEMPLATE.md`.

---

### SLIDE 5: Our Solution
- **Slide Objective**: Introduce the solution concept clearly.
- **Key Message**: *[Product Name]* provides *[Core Mechanism]* to enable *[Primary Outcome]*.
- **Supporting Content**: 3 core value pillars (e.g., Speed, Clarity, Reliability).
- **Visual Suggestion**: High-resolution screenshot or hero mockup of the application interface.

---

### SLIDE 6: How It Works
- **Slide Objective**: Demystify the user journey in 3 simple steps.
- **Key Message**: Simple, seamless end-to-end user experience.
- **Supporting Content**: Step 1 (Input/Trigger) $\rightarrow$ Step 2 (Automated Processing) $\rightarrow$ Step 3 (Actionable Result).
- **Visual Suggestion**: 3-step horizontal process diagram with clean icons.

---

### SLIDE 7: Core MVP Features (Implemented & Working)
- **Slide Objective**: Showcase the working product scope.
- **Key Message**: 4–5 focused, robust, and verified capabilities.
- **Supporting Content**: Concise descriptions of Feature 1, Feature 2, Feature 3, and Supporting Features.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Grid layout with UI screenshot thumbnails and feature callouts.

---

### SLIDE 8: Differentiating Capability (The "WOW" Factor)
- **Slide Objective**: Spotlight the core technological or workflow advantage.
- **Key Message**: The high-leverage mechanism that makes the product uniquely valuable.
- **Supporting Content**: Before vs. After comparison (e.g., 10 manual steps vs. 1 automated action).
- **Visual Suggestion**: Side-by-side comparison graphic or animated GIF of the differentiator.

---

### SLIDE 9: Technical Architecture & Simplicity
- **Slide Objective**: Demonstrate sound engineering, reliability, and security.
- **Key Message**: Lightweight, reliable, and failure-resilient architecture.
- **Supporting Content**: Tech stack breakdown, local data models, input validation, and fallback mechanisms.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Clean component block diagram (`docs/06-architecture/ARCHITECTURE_TEMPLATE.md`).

---

### SLIDE 10: Live Demo Walkthrough
- **Slide Objective**: Transition seamlessly into the live demonstration.
- **Key Message**: "Let's see it in action."
- **Supporting Content**: 3-step live demo sequence overview.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Live application screen / video placeholder (with offline backup state ready).

---

### SLIDE 11: Real-World Impact & Business Case
- **Slide Objective**: Prove practical utility and scalability.
- **Key Message**: Tangible time, cost, or accuracy improvements for end users.
- **Supporting Content**: Projected efficiency gains, adoption feasibility, and user benefits.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Key metric callout cards (e.g., "85% Time Saved", "Zero Setup Latency").

---

### SLIDE 12: Transparent Limitations & Future Roadmap
- **Slide Objective**: Show honest engineering rigor and post-hackathon vision.
- **Key Message**: Strong MVP foundation with clear growth horizons.
- **Supporting Content**:
  - *Current MVP Scope Bounds*: Known edge cases and operational constraints.
  - *Future Vision*: Next iterations (e.g., multi-platform, advanced integrations).
- **Visual Suggestion**: 2-column layout: "Current MVP" vs. "Future Horizons".

---

### SLIDE 13: Summary & Closing
- **Slide Objective**: Deliver a memorable concluding takeaway.
- **Key Message**: *[Product Name]* — The fastest/clearest way to *[Solve Core Problem]*.
- **Supporting Content**: Team credits, GitHub repository link, live demo URL.
- **Visual Suggestion**: Bold closing brand card with QR code or link affordance.
